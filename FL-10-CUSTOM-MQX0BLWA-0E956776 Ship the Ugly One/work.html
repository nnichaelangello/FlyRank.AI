PRISMA (Federated Learning Major Recommendation)

The Problem: Training a machine learning model to recommend university majors requires vast amounts of student academic data, which creates significant privacy and data-sharing risks.

What I Did & Decided: Architected a privacy-preserving recommendation engine using Federated Learning. Used Node.js and Next.js for the platform and implemented a Federated Averaging algorithm with Neural Networks. This allowed the model to train decentrally on local student data without ever transferring raw personal data to the central server.

The Outcome: Successfully demonstrated a secure, scalable way to provide data-driven major recommendations while mathematically guaranteeing user privacy.

AKSARA Learning (AI for Dyslexia)

[ AKSARA Web Interface ]

Real screenshot to be inserted here

The Problem: Students with dyslexia struggle in traditional classroom environments because standard text-based materials lack the multisensory engagement they need to process information effectively.

What I Did & Decided: Developed a web-based inclusive learning platform that adapts to the student. Integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS), Speech-to-Text (STT), and OCR models directly into the workflow to turn static text into interactive, multisensory lessons.

The Outcome: The platform was successfully field-tested and implemented in special needs schools (SLB Bina Mandiri). Won 2nd Place at the National Student Digital Innovation Competition (LIDM 2025).

FlyRank.AI (Search Intelligence)

The Problem: As a machine learning intern, I needed to evaluate how varying dataset features impact predictive accuracy for search intelligence rankings.

What I Did & Decided: Framed the research question, ran extensive baseline experiments using decision tree classifiers, and established a reproducible ML pipeline to evaluate ranking opportunities.

The Outcome: Delivered structured ML task framing and validated baseline models that lay the groundwork for advanced ranking algorithms at FlyRank.
