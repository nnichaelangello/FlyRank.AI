# The Plan to Keep Building

## My Next Case Study
**Name of the Project:** "AI Resume Screener & Skill Matcher"

**The Three-Beat Shape:**
1. **The Problem:** Recruiters receive hundreds of resumes for a single technical role, making it impossible to evaluate each candidate fairly and quickly. Keyword matching systems (ATS) often miss candidates with unconventional phrasing for the same skills.
2. **What I Did:** I will build a lightweight RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline using LangChain and a local LLM to semantically compare applicant resumes (PDFs) against job descriptions.
3. **The Outcome:** A secure, privacy-preserving script that outputs a "Match Score" and a 3-bullet summary of why a candidate fits, drastically reducing screening time while avoiding standard ATS traps.
