# Impact Project: AI Fluency Capstone Summary

This document serves as the final proof of completion for the core directive of the AI Fluency track: *"Master the AI stack, build a personal brand with a real website, ship a personal agent."*

## 1. Master the AI Stack
Throughout this internship, I bridged the gap between raw Machine Learning engineering and AI-assisted web development. On the ML side, I processed massive production datasets (79 million search rows) and successfully identified and prevented critical client-level data leakage using `GroupShuffleSplit`. I translated raw probability scores into actionable business logic for content teams.

## 2. Build a Personal Brand with a Real Website
I built, iterated, and deployed a premium, responsive portfolio. 
- **Tech Stack:** HTML5, CSS3, TailwindCSS, GSAP (Animations), Formspree (Backend).
- **Design Philosophy:** *Frame, not upstage*. Using a sleek dark mode and glassmorphism UI, the website acts as a museum framing my actual work.
- **Judgement:** I actively chose real, authentic screenshots of my past work (AKSARA and PRISMA) over hallucinated, generic AI graphics, proving my ability to ship real software.

## 3. Ship a Personal Agent
I designed and shipped the **Research Scout Agent** in Python. 
- **Capability:** It autonomously queries Google, visits the top URLs, extracts the readable HTML via BeautifulSoup, and compiles the raw text into a JSON file for downstream LLM processing. 
- **Transparency:** I documented its strengths (speed) and its limitations (inability to parse client-side rendered JavaScript), demonstrating engineering honesty.
